# Keyboard shortcuts

> vivePDF keyboard shortcuts, the per-screen list that F1 opens, saving it as a PDF, the macOS key equivalents and the full list for every screen.

URL: https://vivepdf.com/en/docs/shortcuts/
Last updated: 2026-10-18

Most things you do often in vivePDF have a keyboard shortcut: opening a document, searching, zooming, presenting or formatting in Studio. This page groups the shortcuts by screen, and the same list is always one key away inside the app.

## The shortcut list in the app

On any screen, press `F1` or `Ctrl+/` to open the **Shortcuts** window, which shows only the shortcuts that work on that screen. In the **Viewer**, for example, you see the viewing, editing and presentation shortcuts; in the **Page organizer**, the page shortcuts; and in Studio, those of the document or design editor. The general shortcuts that work everywhere are added to each list. Press `F1` during a presentation and the list shows only the presentation shortcuts, so you can find a key without leaving the slides.

You can reach the same window in other ways too:

- **Help › Keyboard shortcuts (F1)** in the top bar.
- **Keyboard shortcuts for this screen** in the command palette, which opens with `Ctrl+K`.

The **Search shortcuts** box at the top narrows the list. The buttons at the bottom do the following:

- **Save as PDF** turns the list on screen into a PDF you can print and keep beside the keyboard.
- **All shortcuts** opens the complete list with every group, whichever screen you are on.
- **Help topics** goes to the help topic for the current screen. `Shift+F1` does the same.

In the Studio design editor, `F1` or `?` opens the editor's own shortcut window. See [Design in Studio](https://vivepdf.com/en/docs/studio-design/) for more.

## Keys on macOS

On a Mac, vivePDF shows its shortcuts with Mac keys:

- `⌘` (Command) takes the place of `Ctrl`, `⌥` (Option) of `Alt`, and `⇧` of `Shift`.
- Full screen is `⌃``⌘``F` instead of `F11`.
- On Mac keyboards, function keys such as `F1` and `F5` may also need `fn`, depending on your keyboard settings.

The **Windows · Linux** and **macOS** buttons just below change which keys the lists show. The full list of shortcuts, grouped by screen, follows below.
