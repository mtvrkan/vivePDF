# Tools

> Where to open vivePDF's 77 tools, what each of the six tool groups is for, and a searchable list with a short description of every tool.

URL: https://vivepdf.com/en/docs/tools/
Last updated: 2026-10-18

vivePDF has 77 tools, from merging and OCR to conversion and digital signatures, all sorted into six groups. This page explains where to open them and what each group is for; the short description of every tool is in the list below.

## Open a tool

There are three ways to reach a tool:

- **Tools menu**: click **Tools** in the top bar. The main tool pages open under six group headings. **Show all** at the bottom of the menu opens the **Tools** catalogue, which lists every tool with a search box, including the sub-tools the menu does not show. **Search in folders** searches inside the PDFs in a folder.
- **Home**: the **Tools** section has a **Search tools** box and a button for each group; choose **All** or a group to narrow the list. To keep the ones you use most in **Quick access**, use **Customize home**.
- **Command palette**: press `Ctrl+K`, or click the **Search tools, pages or documents** box in the top bar, type the tool's name and press `Enter`.

The rest of the top bar and the palette are covered on the [Interface](https://vivepdf.com/en/docs/interface/) page.

## The six groups

- **Page layout**: tools that change the order and number of pages, such as merging, splitting, the page organiser, rotating, deleting and extracting pages. More on the [Organise pages](https://vivepdf.com/en/docs/pages/) page.
- **Optimisation**: tools that improve or check a file, such as compressing, repairing, OCR, comparing, accessibility, print check, PDF/A archiving, batch runs and watched folders.
- **To PDF**: tools that make a PDF from pictures, Office documents, SVG drawings and web pages. The Studio cards for documents, designs, business cards, bibliographies and CVs are here too.
- **From PDF**: tools that turn a PDF into Word, Excel, PowerPoint, pictures, text, Markdown, HTML or EPUB, or pull out its pictures. See [Convert files](https://vivepdf.com/en/docs/convert/).
- **Content editing**: tools that work on what is on the page, such as page numbers, headers and footers, watermarks, cropping, forms, mark-up, QR codes and answer sheets.
- **Security**: adding and removing passwords, sealing with a certificate, redaction, the privacy check, signing and verifying. See [Security](https://vivepdf.com/en/docs/security/).

Every tool follows below, grouped and with a short description; type in the box above the list to find the one you need straight away.
