# Open and read a PDF

> How to open documents in vivePDF, move between pages, search the text, zoom, change the page display, split the window and read comfortably.

URL: https://vivepdf.com/en/docs/open-and-read/
Last updated: 2026-10-18

To open a PDF in vivePDF, click **Open PDF** on Home or in the top bar, or press `Ctrl+O`. The document opens in its own tab in the Viewer, where you can move between pages, search the text and zoom.

## Open a document

1. On Home, click **Open PDF** or press `Ctrl+O`, then choose your file.
2. Or drag files straight onto the window. If you drop several, each one opens in its own tab.
3. If the document is encrypted, **Password required** appears; type the password and the document opens.

You are not limited to PDFs: Office documents, e-books, e-mails and pictures dropped on the window open as a PDF copy. Changes to that copy stay temporary until you keep them with **Save as PDF…**. If you have copied a picture or some text, `Ctrl+Shift+V` turns it into a PDF.

Very large files open too: vivePDF reads the document in pieces instead of loading it into memory all at once.

### Recent documents and sessions

Documents you open stay in the **Recent documents** list on Home. Pin the ones you use often and they are kept in a group of their own at the top. The **Restore last session** action in the command palette brings back the tabs that were open when you last closed the app. When you reopen a document, vivePDF continues where you left off; to read from the start, click **Go to page 1** in the notice.

## Move between pages

- Type a page number in the box at the bottom of the right-hand strip, or use its arrows to go to the previous or next page. If the document has page labels such as iii or A-1, you can type those as well.
- `Page Up`/`Page Down` and the left and right arrow keys change page, and `Home`/`End` go to the first and last page.
- After jumping to another page through a link or the outline, `Alt+←` takes you back and `Alt+→` forward again.
- The **Thumbnails** panel on the left shows every page, and the **Outline** panel lists the document's bookmarks.

## Search the document

`Ctrl+F` opens the search bar. Type the word you are looking for; the results are listed grouped by page. **Match case** and **Whole word** narrow them down. `F3` moves to the next result and `Shift+F3` to the previous one.

> **Searching scanned pages**
>
> Search only works on pages that have a text layer. If a scanned document returns no results, make its pages searchable with [OCR](https://vivepdf.com/en/docs/ocr/) first.

## Zoom and page display

`Ctrl++` and `Ctrl+-`, or the mouse wheel with `Ctrl` held down, zoom in and out. `Ctrl+0` picks **Actual size**, `Ctrl+1` **Fit width** and `Ctrl+2` **Fit page**. To move the page around, hold `Space` and drag.

The **Page display** menu in the right-hand strip offers:

- **Single page**, **Two pages side by side** or **Two pages, cover page alone**
- **Vertical scrolling** or **Horizontal scrolling**
- **Automatic scrolling** (`Ctrl+Shift+H`); while it runs, the up and down arrows change the speed and `Esc` stops it
- **Page colours**: **Sepia**, **Dark (inverted)** or easy-on-the-eyes options such as white, yellow or green text on black

### Split window

`Ctrl+Shift+E` splits the window in two. From **Page display › Split window** you can place the panes **Side by side** or **Stacked**, and **Compare with another document…** opens a different file in the second pane. **Sync scrolling** keeps both panes moving together.

## Comfortable reading

- **Reading mode** shows the text of the page in a single column. You can make the text larger or smaller and choose the column width and the **Paper**, **Sepia** or **Dark** theme.
- **Read aloud** reads the document in the voice and at the speed you choose.
- `F11` switches to immersive mode and shows the document full screen. If you want to present it, see [Present a PDF](https://vivepdf.com/en/docs/present/).
- `Ctrl+P` opens the print dialog.

Every Viewer shortcut is listed on the [Keyboard shortcuts](https://vivepdf.com/en/docs/shortcuts/) page, and the overall layout of the screen is described under [Interface](https://vivepdf.com/en/docs/interface/).
