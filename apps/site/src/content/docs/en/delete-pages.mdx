title: Delete pages from a PDF
description: How to select the pages you don't want in a PDF and delete them with the vivePDF page organizer, then save the result to a new file.
updated: 2026-10-24
---

To delete pages from a PDF, open it, switch to **Pages** in the top bar, select the pages, press <kbd>Delete</kbd> and click **Apply and save**. The result is written to a new file; your original stays as it was.

<Steps>
1. Open the document and click **Pages** in the top bar. Every page is laid out as a thumbnail.
2. Click the page you want to delete. For several pages, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>-click them one by one, <kbd>Shift</kbd>-click a range, or tick the boxes in the corners of the cards.
3. Press <kbd>Delete</kbd> or click the bin icon in the bar at the bottom. The selected pages leave the list at once.
4. Check the **Output file** name on the right and click **Apply and save**.
</Steps>

<Shot src="en/pages-2" alt="The page organizer with a 6-page report; the last page selected and the 1 page selected bar at the bottom" />

Deleted the wrong page? Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd>; nothing is written to the file until you press **Apply and save**.

<Shot src="en/pages-4" alt="The page deleted and saved: 5 pages left, report-edited.pdf and a preview in the panel on the right" />

<Callout type="note" title="Changing the original file instead">
Turn on **Overwrite the original file** to apply the change to the original instead of a new file. This cannot be undone, so leave it off if you are unsure.
</Callout>

The same screen lets you reorder pages by dragging, rotate them, add pages from another PDF and extract the selected pages to a separate file. All of it is on the [Organise pages](/en/docs/pages/) page.
