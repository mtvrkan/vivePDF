To change the text in a PDF, open it, click **Edit** in the strip on the right, click the paragraph you want to change and type. The font, size and colour are in the panel on the right; when you are done, press **Save** or **Save as…**.

<Steps>
1. Open the document in the Viewer and click **Edit** in the strip on the right. **Edit text** is selected in the bar at the top.
2. Click the paragraph you want to change; a frame appears around it. Correct the text as in any text box.
3. To add new text, click an empty spot on the page; a new text box opens there.
4. Change the font, size and colour in the **Edit** panel on the right. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> to undo a step.
5. **Save** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>) writes the change into the file itself; to keep the original, choose **Save as…** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>) and give the new file a name.
</Steps>

The **Layers** list on the right shows every text and picture on the page. The eye icon hides an item, and the lock icon keeps it from being changed by accident.

## Pictures and other items

Switch to **Edit pictures** in the bar at the top to move, resize, replace or delete the pictures on the page. The **Add** menu puts new items on the page, such as a text box, picture, shape, table, chart or formula.

<Callout type="warn" title="Scanned pages have no text to edit">
A scanned page is one big picture. To edit its words, recognise the text with [OCR](/en/docs/ocr/) first.
</Callout>

If you only want to remove some words and cover them with a box nobody can read, use [redaction](/en/docs/redact-a-pdf/) instead of editing; edited text can survive elsewhere in the file.
