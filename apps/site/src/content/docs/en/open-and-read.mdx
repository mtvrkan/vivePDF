To open a PDF in vivePDF, click **Open PDF** on Home or in the top bar, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>O</kbd>. The document opens in its own tab in the Viewer, where you can move between pages, search the text and zoom.

## Open a document

<Steps>
1. On Home, click **Open PDF** or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>O</kbd>, then choose your file.
2. Or drag files straight onto the window. If you drop several, each one opens in its own tab.
3. If the document is encrypted, **Password required** appears; type the password and the document opens.
</Steps>

You are not limited to PDFs: Office documents, e-books, e-mails and pictures dropped on the window open as a PDF copy. Changes to that copy stay temporary until you keep them with **Save as PDF…**. If you have copied a picture or some text, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>V</kbd> turns it into a PDF.

You can also send a Word, Excel or PowerPoint file to vivePDF by right-clicking it in Explorer or Finder and choosing **Open With › vivePDF**. LibreOffice turns Office files into PDFs; when it is not on your computer, vivePDF offers to set it up in the **LibreOffice is needed** window and opens the file once it is ready. LibreOffice is free and only needs setting up once.

Very large files open too: vivePDF reads the document in pieces instead of loading it into memory all at once.

### Recent documents and sessions

Documents you open stay in the **Recent documents** list on Home. Pin the ones you use often and they are kept in a group of their own at the top. The **Restore last session** action in the command palette brings back the tabs that were open when you last closed the app. When you reopen a document, vivePDF continues where you left off; to read from the start, click **Go to page 1** in the notice.

## Move between pages

- Type a page number in the box at the bottom of the right-hand strip, or use its arrows to go to the previous or next page. If the document has page labels such as iii or A-1, you can type those as well.
- <kbd>Page Up</kbd>/<kbd>Page Down</kbd> and the left and right arrow keys change page, and <kbd>Home</kbd>/<kbd>End</kbd> go to the first and last page.
- After jumping to another page through a link or the outline, <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>←</kbd> takes you back and <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>→</kbd> forward again.
- The **Thumbnails** panel on the left shows every page, and the **Outline** panel lists the document's bookmarks.

## Search the document

<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>F</kbd> opens the search bar. Type the word you are looking for; the results are listed grouped by page. **Match case** and **Whole word** narrow them down. <kbd>F3</kbd> moves to the next result and <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>F3</kbd> to the previous one.

<Callout type="note" title="Searching scanned pages">
Search only works on pages that have a text layer. If a scanned document returns no results, make its pages searchable with [OCR](/en/docs/ocr/) first.
</Callout>

## Zoom and page display

<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>+</kbd> and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>-</kbd>, or the mouse wheel with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> held down, zoom in and out. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>0</kbd> picks **Actual size**, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>1</kbd> **Fit width** and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>2</kbd> **Fit page**. To move the page around, hold <kbd>Space</kbd> and drag.

The **Page display** menu in the right-hand strip offers:

- **Single page**, **Two pages side by side** or **Two pages, cover page alone**
- **Vertical scrolling** or **Horizontal scrolling**
- **Automatic scrolling** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>H</kbd>); while it runs, the up and down arrows change the speed and <kbd>Esc</kbd> stops it
- **Page colours**: **Sepia**, **Dark (inverted)** or easy-on-the-eyes options such as white, yellow or green text on black

### Split window

<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>E</kbd> splits the window in two. From **Page display › Split window** you can place the panes **Side by side** or **Stacked**, and **Compare with another document…** opens a different file in the second pane. **Sync scrolling** keeps both panes moving together.

## Comfortable reading

- **Reading mode** shows the text of the page in a single column. You can make the text larger or smaller and choose the column width and the **Paper**, **Sepia** or **Dark** theme.
- **Read aloud** reads the document in the voice and at the speed you choose.
- <kbd>F11</kbd> switches to immersive mode and shows the document full screen. If you want to present it, see [Present a PDF](/en/docs/present/).
- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>P</kbd> opens the print dialog.

Every Viewer shortcut is listed on the [Keyboard shortcuts](/en/docs/shortcuts/) page, and the overall layout of the screen is described under [Interface](/en/docs/interface/).
