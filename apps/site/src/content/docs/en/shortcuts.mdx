Most things you do often in vivePDF have a keyboard shortcut: opening a document, searching, zooming, presenting or formatting in Studio. This page groups the shortcuts by screen, and the same list is always one key away inside the app.

## The shortcut list in the app

On any screen, press <kbd>F1</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>/</kbd> to open the **Shortcuts** window, which shows only the shortcuts that work on that screen. In the **Viewer**, for example, you see the viewing, editing and presentation shortcuts; in the **Page organizer**, the page shortcuts; and in Studio, those of the document or design editor. The general shortcuts that work everywhere are added to each list. Press <kbd>F1</kbd> during a presentation and the list shows only the presentation shortcuts, so you can find a key without leaving the slides.

You can reach the same window in other ways too:

- **Help › Keyboard shortcuts (F1)** in the top bar.
- **Keyboard shortcuts for this screen** in the command palette, which opens with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>.

The **Search shortcuts** box at the top narrows the list. The buttons at the bottom do the following:

- **Save as PDF** turns the list on screen into a PDF you can print and keep beside the keyboard.
- **All shortcuts** opens the complete list with every group, whichever screen you are on.
- **Help topics** goes to the help topic for the current screen. <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>F1</kbd> does the same.

In the Studio design editor, <kbd>F1</kbd> or <kbd>?</kbd> opens the editor's own shortcut window. See [Design in Studio](/en/docs/studio-design/) for more.

## Keys on macOS

On a Mac, vivePDF shows its shortcuts with Mac keys:

- <kbd>⌘</kbd> (Command) takes the place of <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>, <kbd>⌥</kbd> (Option) of <kbd>Alt</kbd>, and <kbd>⇧</kbd> of <kbd>Shift</kbd>.
- Full screen is <kbd>⌃</kbd><kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>F</kbd> instead of <kbd>F11</kbd>.
- On Mac keyboards, function keys such as <kbd>F1</kbd> and <kbd>F5</kbd> may also need <kbd>fn</kbd>, depending on your keyboard settings.
