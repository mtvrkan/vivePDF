Everything in vivePDF starts from one bar along the top of the window. The **Home**, **Viewer**, **Pages** and **Studio** buttons on the left switch between workspaces, and the **Tools** and **Help** menus hold the rest. The documents you open sit in tabs, which you can group or move into separate windows.

## The top bar

The vivePDF logo and the four workspaces sit on the left of the bar:

- **Home**: recent documents, collections and quick tools. **Customize home** lets you rearrange its sections.
- **Viewer**: where you read, search and mark up documents. See [Open and read a PDF](/en/docs/open-and-read/).
- **Pages**: the page organizer, showing every page of the open document as a thumbnail. See [Organise pages](/en/docs/pages/).
- **Studio**: an editor for writing a document or making a design from a blank page.

On the right you will find the search box that opens the command palette, the theme button (it switches between light and dark), the **Language** menu, **Settings** (the gear icon) and the **Open PDF** button. When a new version is ready, a green update button appears to the left of the search box.

As the window gets narrower the bar compacts itself: labels go first, then the menus turn into icons, so search, settings and **Open PDF** stay visible at any width.

## The Tools menu

Click **Tools** and the tools open in six groups:

- **Page layout**: Merge, Split, Rename
- **Optimisation**: Compress, OCR, Scans, Compare, Accessibility, Print check, PDF/A archive, Batch, Watched folders
- **To PDF**: Create PDF
- **From PDF**: Convert
- **Content editing**: Edit, QR & barcodes, Answer sheets, Forms
- **Security**: Security, Sign

**Show all** at the bottom of the menu opens the catalogue of every tool with a search box; tools that have no line of their own in the menu, such as rotating pages or adding a watermark, are listed there. **Search in folders** searches the text of every PDF in the folders you have indexed and jumps straight to the page. The full list is on the [Tools](/en/docs/tools/) page.

## The command palette

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>, or click the **Search tools, pages or documents** box in the top bar, to open the command palette. Type the name of a tool, a settings section or an open document, pick it with the arrow keys and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

Start your search with a symbol to narrow the palette to one kind of result:

- `>` shows only actions, such as **New window**, **PDF from clipboard** or **Check for updates**.
- `/` searches pages and settings sections.
- `@` searches open documents and recent files.
- `#` followed by a number goes to that page of the open document.

## The Help menu

The **Help** menu holds **Help topics**, **Keyboard shortcuts (F1)**, **What's new** and **Privacy**. Below them come **Report a bug**, **Suggest a feature**, **Open log folder** and **Save support bundle…**, and at the bottom **Check for updates** and **About**.

<Callout type="tip" title="Shortcuts for this screen">
Wherever you are, <kbd>F1</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>/</kbd> shows the shortcuts for that screen, and <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>F1</kbd> opens its help topic. The full list is on the [Keyboard shortcuts](/en/docs/shortcuts/) page.
</Callout>

## Tabs and tab groups

Every document you open gets its own tab above the Viewer; the same tabs also appear in Pages. Drag a tab to change its place, or move it with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>←</kbd>/<kbd>→</kbd>. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>W</kbd> closes the active document.

Right-click a tab for options such as **Close others**, **Close all**, **Copy path**, **Copy file name**, **Show in folder** and **Open beside current document**. **Add to new group** in the same menu groups tabs the way a web browser does:

- Click the name chip in front of a group to collapse or expand it, double-click it to rename the group, and drag it to move the whole group.
- The group menu offers **Colour** (13 colours), **Ungroup**, **Close the group's documents**, **Open a document in this group** and **Save as collection**.
- A group saved as a collection appears under **Collections** on Home, where you can later reopen all its files as one tab group.

## Windows

<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd> opens an empty new window. To give a document a window of its own, drag its tab out of the window and let go, or choose **Move to new window** from the tab menu; **Move group to new window** in the group menu moves all of the group's documents to a new window. A document with unsaved changes stays in its window until you save it. Settings, theme, language and recent files are shared by every window.

## Side panels in the Viewer

In the Viewer, a vertical strip of buttons runs down each side of the page.

The **Navigation** strip on the left opens the **Thumbnails**, **Outline** and **Attachments** panels. A **Signatures** button appears when the document is signed, and **Layers** when it has layers.

The **Tools** strip on the right starts with **Comments**, **Annotate / mark up**, **Edit** and **Presentation tools**. Then come **Reading mode**, **Read aloud**, **Page colours** and **Document info**. At the bottom of the strip are the page number box, **Rotate**, the **Page display** menu and the zoom buttons.

Each button opens and closes its own panel. You can choose which panels are ready when a document opens in **Settings › Viewer**; for the other settings see [Settings and privacy](/en/docs/settings-privacy/).
