To sign a PDF, open it, click **Edit** in the strip on the right, choose **Page tools › Place signature**, draw your signature and place it on the page. The signed document is saved as a new file; your original stays as it was.

<Steps>
1. Open the document in the Viewer and click **Edit** in the strip on the right.
2. In the bar at the top, choose **Place signature** from the **Page tools** menu.
3. Click **New** and draw your signature with a mouse, pen or finger. To use a photo of your signature on paper, choose **From photo**. Click **Save signature**.
4. Click where the signature goes, drag it into place and change its size with the **Width** slider.
5. Click **Apply signature**.
</Steps>

<Shot src="en/sign-1" alt="The Signatures window: a signature drawn in the Draw a signature area, with Clear, From photo and Save signature" />

A saved signature is ready next time, so you don't have to draw it again. It is kept on this computer and never sent anywhere.

<Shot src="en/sign-3" alt="The signature placed on the Client line of the agreement; the Width slider and Apply signature in the bar at the top" />

After **Apply signature**, the signed copy (for example `agreement-signed.pdf`) opens in a new tab.

<Shot src="en/sign-4" alt="The signed agreement open in a new tab, with a Signed: agreement-signed.pdf message in the corner" />

<Callout type="note" title="A drawn signature is not a digital signature">
A drawn signature is a picture on the page; it does not prove the document was not changed afterwards. For formal documents you can sign with a .p12 or .pfx certificate; see [Sign and verify](/en/docs/sign/).
</Callout>
