vivePDF has 86 tools, from merging and OCR to conversion and digital signatures, all sorted into thirteen categories. This page explains where to open them and what each category is for; the short description of every tool is in the list below.

## Open a tool

There are three ways to reach a tool:

- **Tools menu**: click **Tools** in the top bar. The categories are listed on the left with the number of tools in each; point at one and its tools appear on the right with a short description. **Show all** at the bottom of the menu opens the **Tools** catalogue, which lists every tool with a search box. **Search in folders** searches inside the PDFs in a folder.
- **Home**: the **Tools** section has a **Search tools** box and a button for each group; choose **All** or a group to narrow the list. To keep the ones you use most in **Quick access**, use **Customize home**.
- **Command palette**: press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>, or click the **Search tools, pages or documents** box in the top bar, type the tool's name and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

The rest of the top bar and the palette are covered on the [Interface](/en/docs/interface/) page.

## The thirteen categories

- **Page layout**: tools that change the order, number and size of pages: merging, splitting, the page organiser, rotating, deleting and extracting pages, cropping, page size, booklets and posters. More on the [Organise pages](/en/docs/pages/) page.
- **Optimisation**: compressing, repairing, OCR, flattening and PDF/A archiving.
- **Check**: comparing two documents, accessibility, print check, the thesis check and the privacy check.
- **Scan**: scanning from a scanner or a photo, cleaning up scans and splitting a scanned batch into documents.
- **To PDF**: tools that make a PDF from pictures, Office documents, SVG drawings and web pages.
- **From PDF**: tools that turn a PDF into Word, Excel, PowerPoint, pictures, SVG, text, Markdown, HTML or EPUB, or pull out its pictures. See [Convert files](/en/docs/convert/).
- **Create & design**: writing a document and designing in Studio, Create PDF, CVs, business cards, books, documents from a table, paper and bibliographies.
- **Content editing**: tools that work on what is on the page: editing text, find and replace, page numbers, headers and footers, cover pages, letterheads, background colour, bookmarks, automatic links and document info.
- **Marks & codes**: adding and removing watermarks, stamps, adding QR codes and reading codes.
- **Forms & exams**: filling forms, mail merge, exporting form data as a table, detecting fields, and creating and grading answer sheets.
- **Read & review**: mark-up, comments, attachments, reading aloud, translating and printing.
- **Security & signing**: signing and verifying, adding and removing passwords, redaction, sealing and opening with a certificate, and creating and exporting certificates. See [Security](/en/docs/security/).
- **Automation**: batch runs, watched folders and renaming files.

Every tool follows below, by category and with a short description; type in the box above the list to find the one you need straight away.
